GTAO

GUIDE · rendering · AONode · living document

The Forward+ ambient-occlusion node produces world-space bent normals in RGB and visibility in alpha. Material AO still combines with visibility using min(); IBL retains its multibounce and specular-occlusion responses.

Configuration

The AmbientOcclusion node accepts these render-graph settings:

  "id": "AmbientOcclusion",
  "type": "AmbientOcclusion",
  "enabled": true,
  "quality": "medium",
  "temporal": true,
  "output": "View.GTAO"
}

A preset changes noise, never the amount of occlusion. Steps per side is the same for every preset and is not a field of the preset table. The sweep marks the sectors its taps land in, so its horizon is a maximum over samples: occlusion rises with the tap count and does not converge. Measured on the GPU over three synthetic fixtures, three / four / six / twelve / twenty-four taps gave 0.027 / 0.033 / 0.046 / 0.064 / 0.077 mean occlusion on the same frame — a preset that raised the tap count would darken every frame and silently retune every AmbientOcclusion volume. Resolution and slice count are neutral in amount (within 10 % and 1.5 % respectively) and are therefore what the presets buy: Medium to High is sharpness, High to Ultra is less slice banding. GtaoComputeTest.PresetsAgreeOnOcclusionAmount holds the three presets to 10 % of each other on the GPU, and the tap count lives in kGtaoSweepSteps.
There is no low preset. A fixed per-frame cost — prepare, the depth mip chain, the denoiser and the temporal pass, about 0.105 ms of the Medium chain on an RTX 5080 at 3067×900 — dominates below Medium, so a 3-slice preset measured 13 % cheaper for 40 % fewer taps. A cheaper tier has to remove passes, not taps; until one does, Medium is the floor.

Radius, thickness, and intensity remain PostProcessVolume properties. Invalid quality names fail node initialization. With temporal: false, the noise phase is fixed and no history pass or resources are allocated. AO intensity zero disables the node and invalidates its history.

Existing volumes may need their Intensity re-tuned. This estimator puts occlusion on contact geometry rather than spreading a tint over whole objects, so the same Intensity does not reproduce the same picture as the single-pass node it replaces. Tune Intensity against a scene, not against a number carried over.

The Inspector has no control for quality or temporal accumulation. Edit the quality and temporal fields on the AmbientOcclusion node in the rendergraph asset used by your camera's pipeline, using the values above. Edit the source asset, not a staged build copy.

Limits

GTAO runs before object motion vectors are available. Reprojection therefore handles camera motion; conservative rejection limits stale occlusion on moving objects but does not provide object-motion reprojection. The temporal toggle is useful when evaluating rapidly moving or deforming geometry. Letterboxed subrect views use the unoccluded fallback because their depth UV mapping differs.

On the web (WebGPU), an adapter without the float32-filterable feature leaves the effect inactive, and the log says so once.
